# product-wiki

Self-growing, agent-maintained research on Claudinite's product, users, market
and technology — comedians recording, segmenting, managing and reviewing
comedy bits, on iPad first. Compiled once per wiki, refined in place, every
claim cited (see the `product-wiki` pack's `RULES.md`).

Walled off from the rest of the repo: nothing here is a dependency until a
human reviews it into `product-requirements/`, the one crossing point.

## Wikis

- [`audience/`](audience/README.md) — who the app is for, and the two jobs it
  does for them (working new material; tracking/managing existing material).
- [`market/`](market/README.md) — competing and adjacent apps for comedians.
- [`technology/`](technology/README.md) — recording, transcription, laugh/audio
  analysis, and text segmentation approaches the app could build on.

## product-requirements/

The human-reviewed distillation of the wikis above into product requirements.
Never auto-grown — see [`product-requirements/README.md`](product-requirements/README.md).

No growth pass has run yet — these wikis are seeded skeletons only. The first
research pass is deferred to a later session.
