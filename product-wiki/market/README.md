# market

Apps and tools comedians use today to record, organize, and review material —
competitors and adjacent alternatives Claudinite is measured against.

## Key insights

- The default toolkit is phone Voice Memos plus a notes app; listening back is the manual, high-effort step.
- A direct competitor exists: StandApp Comedy transcribes an uploaded set, splits it into bits, tracks laughs-per-minute.
- No competitor found does live on-device recording with automatic laugh detection, on iPad or anywhere.
- Comedy-specific apps are numerous, small, writing/setlist-first — and mostly free; paying for tools is unproven here.
- Comedy educators themselves dispute laughs-per-minute as a performance metric.
- Descript's pattern transfers directly: the transcript as the interface to the recording.
- Jokely and BitBinder now advertise automatic transcription and joke-success analytics — "the writing apps just record" no longer holds.

## Direct competitors

Research caveat: page fetches were egress-blocked in the research environment, so
every claim below is attributed from search snippets naming the publisher, not
from opened pages. Pricing, platform, ratings and last-update details need
page-level verification by a human or an unblocked environment.

- **StandApp Comedy** ([standappcomedy.com](https://standappcomedy.com/)) — the closest
  direct competitor: per its own site, "drop in a recording of your set and instantly
  track your Laughs Per Minute (LPM)", shows where laughs hit, transcribes the set and
  splits it into bits, 90+ languages, organizes sets/bits with dates, tags and stats.
  Upload-based rather than live-recording; platform (web vs. mobile) and pricing not
  visible in snippets.
- **Comedy Metric** ([comedymetric.app](https://comedymetric.app/)) — "data-driven
  performance platform… track laughs per minute, analyze sets, time your acts, manage
  shows, book gigs." Second LPM competitor; unclear whether LPM is measured or
  self-reported.
- **Comedy Evaluator Pro** ([comedyevaluatorpro.com](https://www.comedyevaluatorpro.com/)) —
  long-standing web tool measuring "Positive Audience Response" (laughter/cheering/applause)
  from recordings; appears manual/semi-manual rather than ML, sold as non-recurring
  on-demand access after a trial. Site content reads 2010s-era.
- **Jokely** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/jokely-stand-up-comedy/id6744562322),
  [jokelyapp.com](https://jokelyapp.com/)) — iOS + Android joke/set writing with a Stage
  Mode (clean on-stage view, timer, auto-records the performance) and "Post-Show
  Reflection & Analytics… analyze audience reactions". Free download. **Updated
  2026-09-06**: its own listing now claims more than passive storage — "automatically
  transcribe, and spot timing or delivery issues quickly… track laugh-metrics over
  time" — superseding the "unverified whether it analyzes or just stores" note below;
  whether that's real audio ML or a rebrand of manual tagging is itself unverified
  (App Store page fetch still blocked — snippet only, same as the rest of this
  section).
- **The Comedy Companion** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/the-comedy-companion/id1245335115),
  [AppBrain](https://www.appbrain.com/app/the-comedy-companion/com.comedycompanion)) —
  iOS + Android; jokes → setlists → performance tracking with audio recording of shows;
  free. A review notes clunky navigation. Its React Native codebase is public
  ([github.com/dereksweet/ComedyCompanion](https://github.com/dereksweet/ComedyCompanion)).
- **BitBinder** ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/hn/app/bitbinder/id6753145615)) —
  by comedian Taylor Drew; "100% local, no sign-in, no tracking" (the audience page's
  data-custody citation). **Updated 2026-09-06**: its listing also now claims
  post-set analysis, not just capture — "review recordings with precise notes, track
  joke success rates over time, analyze audience reactions by material" — the same
  supersession as Jokely's, snippet-only and unverified.
- **Writing-first indie cluster** — Comedy OS, Standup Studio, Standup Writer, Bits
  ([thebits.club](https://www.thebits.club/)), Jokebook, Joktor, Show Your
  Bits, JokeStack ([jokestack.com](https://www.jokestack.com/)), UpMic
  ([upmic.app](https://upmic.app/en)), Stand-Up Writer ([stand.app](https://stand.app/en)),
  Set Lists Pro, Crush: setlist/joke management, not recording analysis. Several App
  Store IDs suggest 2024–2025 launches — indies are actively re-entering the category.
- **Name checks that came up empty or different**: Tight Five is a practice
  *timer* app (£4.99 one-time, club-light simulation —
  [tightfive.overpass.co.uk](https://tightfive.overpass.co.uk/)); LaughLab
  ([laughlab.app](https://laughlab.app/en-CA)) is comedy education; "Joke Journal" exists
  only as an AI joke *generator* (Jokify); Punch-Line App is a collaborative
  joke-voting community; The Humor Index scores *sitcom scripts* by AI, not live sets —
  a different product category; Stand-Up Sitdown, ComicMind, Comedy Gym and Bit
  Tracker — nothing citable found.
- **The open niche narrows, doesn't close** (inference across the snippets above,
  needs page-level verification): the LPM tools are still upload-based post-hoc, and
  Jokely/BitBinder's own marketing now claims post-set analysis rather than pure
  capture — but nothing found claims *live, on-device* laugh detection, and nothing
  targets iPad specifically. The gap this product would fill is narrower than the
  2026-08-20 pass found it, not gone.

## Adjacent / substitute tools

- **Voice Memos + notes app** is the real incumbent: craft literature teaches "tape and
  listen to every set"; working comics describe recording every set on a phone and
  keeping iPhone Notes lists like "Jokes That Work"
  ([funnyhow.substack.com](https://funnyhow.substack.com/p/tape-and-listen-to-every-set),
  [isabelhagen.substack.com](https://isabelhagen.substack.com/p/some-new-material);
  snippet only). Aziz Ansari reportedly listens to every set and hand-annotates a
  notebook (same publisher).
- **Songwriter voice-memo apps** are the structural twin (capture → tag → organize →
  review): Hum ($2.99 one-time), Dubnote (free + $24.99/yr), Spit Notes ($4.99
  lifetime + $1.99/mo AI plan) — cheap one-time or low-annual pricing, metadata
  attached to the recording at capture time
  ([songfancy.com](https://songfancy.com/5-awesome-recording-apps-and-programs-for-beginning-songwriters/),
  [dubnote.com](https://dubnote.com/blog/best-voice-memo-apps-for-musicians),
  [getspitnotes.com](https://getspitnotes.com/blog/best-apps-for-recording-lyrics-and-melodies/);
  snippet only).
- **Descript / Otter** — Descript's core transferable idea is text-based editing: edit
  the transcript and the audio follows; Otter's is passive capture with speaker labels
  and summaries ([descript.com](https://www.descript.com/blog/article/how-to-use-descript),
  [otter.ai](https://otter.ai/blog/descript-alternatives); snippet only). No citable
  evidence surfaced of comedians adopting either for sets.
- **Pricing reality** — free dominates comedian tools (JokeStack "free forever", UpMic
  "completely free", The Comedy Companion, stand.app); where money is charged it is
  small: Tight Five £4.99 one-time, Mint Comedy $5.99/mo
  ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/sc/app/mint-comedy/id1510595505)); the $9.99/wk
  "Comedy App Stand Up Comedians" is content streaming, not a tool. No citable evidence
  of comedians paying meaningful subscriptions for a tool (absence-of-evidence note).
- **The metric is contested** — a craft-advice publisher argues against counting laughs
  per minute as a performance measure
  ([realfirststeps.com](https://www.realfirststeps.com/13528/stand-up-comedy-laughs-per-minute/);
  snippet only) — worth weighing when positioning against the LPM competitors.
