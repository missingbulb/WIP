# audience

Who Claudinite is for, and the two jobs the app does for them: working on new
comedy material (first use) and tracking/managing existing material (second
use).

## Key insights

- Recording every set is canonical advice; listening back is the acknowledged failure point — review costs a set's full runtime.
- Comedians' own recording is seen as theft *protection*; audience recording as the threat — the app sits on the trusted side.
- A competitor already markets "100% local, no sign-in, works offline" — data custody reads as a purchase driver here.
- No official headcount of comedians exists; circulating figures contradict by orders of magnitude.
- Active regulars per metro number in the hundreds, not thousands — the reachable market is niche.
- New-material vs. managing-material maps to career stage; tracking intensifies for touring comics building an hour.

- **No official count exists.** The U.S. BLS folds comedians into "Actors" (~57,000
  jobs, 2024) and "Entertainers and Performers, All Other" — no standalone comedian
  figure ([bls.gov OES](https://www.bls.gov/oes/2023/may/oes272099.htm),
  [bls.gov OOH Actors](https://www.bls.gov/ooh/entertainment-and-sports/actors.htm)).
  Aggregator figures contradict by five orders of magnitude: Zippia's "240 US
  comedians" (job-posting-derived) vs. a "12,000 full-time US comedians" figure whose
  origin publisher nobody names — both unusable
  ([zippia.com](https://www.zippia.com/stand-up-comedian-jobs/demographics/),
  [worldmetrics.org](https://worldmetrics.org/comedy-industry-statistics/),
  [zipdo.co](https://zipdo.co/comedy-industry-statistics/)).
- **The pyramid is wide at the bottom, thin everywhere else**: editorial claims that
  under 1% of open-mic comics ever earn money
  ([Medium/ReThink Reviews](https://medium.com/rethink-reviews/stand-up-comics-are-born-at-open-mics-792100692c5b));
  a Dallas comedian counted fewer than 250 people doing stand-up even monthly in a
  metro of 8M+ ([scottbedgood.substack.com](https://scottbedgood.substack.com/p/one-of-the-few)).
  Open-mic'ers outnumber working comics by orders of magnitude, but active regulars
  per metro are hundreds, not thousands.
- **UK**: the UK Live Comedy Sector Survey (Live Comedy Association + Centre for
  Comedy Studies Research + British Comedy Guide; 2nd edition Feb 2026; 366
  professionals surveyed, two-thirds comedians; sector ~£1bn/yr per Chortle's
  coverage) is the best sizing instrument but publishes no comedian headcount
  ([comedy.co.uk](https://www.comedy.co.uk/live/news/8631/live-comedy-sector-survey-2025),
  [chortle.co.uk](https://www.chortle.co.uk/news/2025/02/06/57454/comedys_a_%C2%A31bn-a-year_industry..._but_not_everything_is_rosy),
  [comedysurvey.co.uk](https://comedysurvey.co.uk/)).
- **Comedy writers look like a poor primary segment**: WGA West ~17,000 / WGA East
  ~7,592 members with no comedy breakdown, and nothing ties staff writers to the
  record-a-live-set workflow
  ([influencewatch.org](https://www.influencewatch.org/labor-union/writers-guild-of-america-west/),
  [Wikipedia/WGAE](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Writers_Guild_of_America_East)).
- **The two jobs map to career stage.** The record-transcribe-iterate loop is
  preached at developing comics; marquee names describe a new-hour cycle (legal pad
  on stage, rough experiments, then archive). The same person alternates modes, and
  cross-show tracking intensifies for touring comics building toward a taping
  ([thecomicscomic.com](https://thecomicscomic.com/2019/02/01/gary-gulmans-comedy-tips/),
  [deadant.co](https://deadant.co/all-365-of-gary-gulmans-writing-tips/),
  [prowritingaid.com](https://prowritingaid.com/art/614/7-Things-We-Can-Learn-From-Jerry-Seinfeld-About-Writing-.aspx)).

## Jobs to be done

- **Job 1 — working new material.** The community's reference text is Gary Gulman's
  tip: "Record every set. The hard part: Listen to it and transcribe everything you
  want to say again" ([thecomicscomic.com](https://thecomicscomic.com/2019/02/01/gary-gulmans-comedy-tips/),
  [funnyordie.com](https://funnyordie.com/2019/01/29/89218/comedy-icon-gary-gulman-is-tweeting-joke-writing-tips-every-comedian-should-read/)).
  Working comics record on phone voice memos and mine recordings for accidents — the
  spontaneous tag, the setup five words shorter that still works
  ([isabelhagen.substack.com](https://isabelhagen.substack.com/p/some-new-material),
  [funnyhow.substack.com](https://funnyhow.substack.com/p/tape-and-listen-to-every-set)).
- **The listening-back gap is the wedge.** No quantitative fraction exists, but the
  qualitative pattern is consistent: recording near-universal, review the failure
  point — "I hate listening back to sets. It's so tedious"; the anti-pattern "I never
  listen. I don't even know why I record them"; Aziz Ansari's listen-to-every-set
  discipline cited as *exceptional*. Named frictions: own voice, hearing silence
  after jokes, scrubbing full-length audio
  ([thecomicscomic.com](https://thecomicscomic.com/2019/02/01/gary-gulmans-comedy-tips/),
  [comedypreneur.com](https://www.comedypreneur.com/how-to-shake-off-a-bad-set/),
  [standup101.substack.com](https://standup101.substack.com/p/precious)).
- **Job 2 — tracking/managing material.** The archive today is physical or
  generic-digital: Seinfeld's accordion folders of legal pads, Birbiglia's journals +
  index cards, phone notes, Google Docs, Notion templates; transferring notebook
  scraps into a computer is manual block-scheduled labor
  ([razajafri.com](https://razajafri.com/blog/how-comedians-develop-material),
  [carylittlejohn.com](https://www.carylittlejohn.com/mike-birbiglias-wisdom-on-journals/),
  [Goodreads/Forte](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/18909372-second-brain-case-study-using-digital-notes-for-stand-up-comedy)).
  A small iPhone-first indie app category (BitBinder, Joktor, Bits, Stand-Up Writer,
  Standup Studio) validates the job but none advertises transcription, laugh
  detection, or cross-show bit analytics
  ([BitBinder](https://apps.apple.com/hn/app/bitbinder/id6753145615),
  [Joktor](https://apps.apple.com/ca/app/joktor/id6520393668),
  [Bits](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/bits-stand-up-comedy-writing/id1481029465)).
- **Trust with unreleased material.** Joke-theft fear is real and behavior-shaping —
  enforcement is social and severe ([daily.jstor.org](https://daily.jstor.org/policing-joke-theft),
  [cracked.com](https://www.cracked.com/article_29739_5-harsh-realities-of-joke-theft-a-stand-up-comics-view.html),
  [bigbencomedy.com](https://www.bigbencomedy.com/archives/stealing-jokes-my-thoughts/)).
  Audience recording is treated as the threat (Yondr phone pouches at Chappelle/Rock/
  Hart/Mulaney shows to keep untested material offline —
  [overyondr.com](https://www.overyondr.com/phone-free-comedy-shows),
  [fox5ny.com](https://www.fox5ny.com/news/dave-chappelle-other-artists-make-fans-lock-phones-in-pouches-during-show),
  [dallasobserver.com](https://www.dallasobserver.com/arts/dallas-and-denton-comedians-dont-want-you-recording-their-sets-10970222)),
  while one's *own* recording is framed as authorship proof
  ([Goodreads/Steve Roye](https://www.goodreads.com/author_blog_posts/4530543-record-for-your-own-protection)).
  BitBinder leading its marketing with "100% local, no sign-in required, works
  offline" is the strongest citable signal that data custody drives purchase here.
